PS & Co.: natural, clean, made with care.

An identity and online store for PS & Co., a family-run business making small-batch soaps, candles and body care and offering spa services.

Client
PS & Co.
Scope
Identity, e-commerce
Platform
Next.js, Stripe
Year
2026

The identity

An engraved botanical sprig sits on a fine rule above a widely spaced serif wordmark, with the promise Natural · Clean · Made with care. It's quiet and handmade in feel, so it suits a soap label, a candle and a spa menu equally well.

The store

The website sells the full product line with a cart and Stripe checkout, free US shipping over $75, and a separate section for booking spa services. The owners manage products themselves through a simple admin page.

OrbitCoat Painting: a brighter Florida ahead.

A complete identity for OrbitCoat Painting, a retro-inspired painting company in St. Cloud, Florida. OrbitCoat pairs quality paint with the kind of service and hospitality customers remember, and the brand needed to say both at a glance, on a van door as clearly as on a business card.

Client
OrbitCoat Painting
Location
St. Cloud, FL
Services
Identity, applications
Year
2026

The idea

OrbitCoat sells more than a fresh coat. The company is built on doing the job right and treating every home and business like a guest. The identity borrows the warmth and confidence of 1950s roadside signage, with a boomerang-shaped sign, starbursts and a brush that leaves an orbit trail, so the brand feels friendly, local and proud of its work, and stands out from the street.

The system

A script wordmark paired with spaced capitals for Painting, the line Higher standards. Brighter spaces., a five-color palette, starburst and planet icon elements and four service icons for interior, exterior, residential and commercial work and statewide service.

  • Atomic Teal#2D7F7B
  • Sunburst Orange#F05A28
  • Cream#F6EAD7
  • Jet Black#080808
  • Space Gray#8A8F94

Applications

Vehicle wrap, crew uniform and cap, yard sign, business cards, paint can label and an illustrated campaign poster: A Brighter Florida Ahead.

Black Paranoia: Case File 3PK.

Pack 001 of the Classified Collection for Black Paranoia, a Baltimore clothing brand inspired by the surveillance state we live in now. Its message: information is power, most of it is hidden, and the aware see what others overlook.

Client
Black Paranoia
Location
Baltimore, MD
Scope
Collection, print, packaging
Year
2026

The concept

Each tee is treated as a document in a case file. Stay Aware carries the eye mark and the line "Everyone looks. Few observe." Redacted Truth hides its message under redaction bars. Observed turns the eye into the O and closes with a subject-and-status label on the back.

Made to be seen

The prints use reflective silver heat-transfer vinyl, so the redaction bars read flat grey by day and flash white under a camera flash at night. The palette stays tight: reflective silver, white and a safety lime accent, on heavyweight cotton in an oversized fit.

  • Reflective Silver#B9B9B9
  • White#F2F2F2
  • Safety Lime#A8A93A

Packaging

A black resealable pouch and a case-file hang tag list the three tees as entries 01 to 03, so opening the pack feels like opening a file.

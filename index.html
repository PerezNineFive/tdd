About

The designer who broke out of the machine.

"I spent over a decade building design systems inside a public utility, where getting it wrong has real consequences."

I'm Tony. Philadelphia-born, with twelve years of institutional design thinking now aimed at brands that want to actually mean something.

I hold a bachelor's degree in Multimedia Design and a master's degree in Human Factors Psychology. Multimedia design gave me the craft: type, motion, interface and image working as one system. Human factors psychology is the study of how people perceive, pay attention, remember and make decisions, and it shapes every brand I build. I design marks that register at a glance, systems that stay easy to read under real-world conditions, and messaging that cuts down the mental work it takes to choose you. Instead of guessing what will land, I design around how people actually see and decide.

I've shipped design systems for a utility serving 500,000+ customers, led go-to-market visual strategy for an AI-powered SaaS platform, and built identities for founders who are done blending in. I bring institutional rigor to personal brands and street-level instinct to enterprise work.

Process

The countdown to launch.

Every project runs the same five-phase sequence. It protects your investment, keeps us aligned and ends with your brand in orbit.

Mission clockT–4Discovery

    Discovery

    A deep dive into your business, market, audience and ambitions. We agree on what success looks like before anything gets designed.

    You getBrand audit, competitor map, positioning brief, creative direction doc.
  2. T–3

    Strategy

    We define your position, voice and story. It's the foundation everything else stands on, and the phase most agencies skip.

    You getStrategy deck, messaging framework, visual direction board.
  3. T–2

    Design

    Concepts, refinement and presentation. You see two or three distinct directions before we commit to the right one.

    You get3 initial concepts, 2 rounds of refinement, final identity system.
